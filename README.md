# *Finding the Perfect Product*
## Description/Motivation


Often, when buying a hair product at the store, we get lured in by the catchy advertisement and keywords they use. They claim to make your hair grow faster, to make it look shiny in just one use, and promise the cure to all your hair problems in one bottle. We spend thousands of dollars to find out that a product does not even work on our hair. In the long term, it becomes expensive and wasteful. We fail to realize that the secret lies in the ingredient list of products and not the buzzwords businesses use. I aim to develop a website that will tell you if a product will work for you based on your hair texture, porosity, and the product's ingredient list. Ideally, it would also suggest what ingredients would benefit your type of hair and make product recommendations.
